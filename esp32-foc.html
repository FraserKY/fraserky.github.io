Field-oriented control on an ESP32

Driving a brushless motor from an ESP32 with field-oriented control, written against ESP-IDF and the chip’s MCPWM peripheral rather than an off-the-shelf library. Early days: the PWM timer, operator and comparator are up, the control maths is not.
